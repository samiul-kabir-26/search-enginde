# Offline eval, 2026-10-27, demo tenant config v5

Suites: text, image, ranking; the active version.

> **v5 vs v6.** v5 is the demo tenant's active config: learned `prerank-gbdt-v1` (ONNX) and
> `rank-gbdt-v1` (Triton) from M7. v6 is the same document with `default_linear` in both stages,
> published only for this comparison (not active; `2026-10-27_v6.md`). With identical
> retrieval, the learned models lose on text relevance (nDCG@10 0.369 vs 0.538) and badly on
> image search (recall@10 0.089 vs 0.553): they were trained on text sessions only, so on image
> queries, where every keyword feature is 0, they rank by popularity and push the visually
> similar products down (traced: the target is in retrieval's top 10 for 20 of 49 queries and
> in pre-rank's for 4). On the simulated users' hidden utility they tie (0.831 vs 0.827).
